oboku is a free and open-source ebook and comic reader that runs in your browser. Connect the places where your books already live, such as Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, a WebDAV server or a Synology NAS, and oboku builds your library from them, with your reading progress synced across your devices. Use the cloud version at [app.oboku.me](https://app.oboku.me) or [self-host it](https://docs.oboku.me/self-hosting/installation) with Docker.

## Features

- Reads EPUB, comics and manga (CBZ, CBR), PDF, plain text, and ZIP or RAR archives, with [Prose](https://github.com/mbret/prose-reader) as its reading engine
- Synchronizes books from Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, WebDAV and Synology, or from the server's own files when self-hosted
- Offline-first progressive web app for phones, tablets, desktops and e-ink devices
- Collections, tags and reading progress, plus password-protected content for books you want to keep hidden
- E-ink mode with higher contrast and no animations
- Free, ad-free and open source
