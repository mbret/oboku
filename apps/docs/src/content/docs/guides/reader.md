The reader part of oboku is built upon [https://prose-reader.com/](https://prose-reader.com/) and its [https://doc.prose-reader.com/react-reader](https://doc.prose-reader.com/react-reader/introduction).

Being the same maintainer for both project, one benefit the other and are usually developed in alternance.

## Colors

The reader takes the **Color mode** of the **Theming** settings: dark in **Dark**, white in **Light** and **E-ink**. In **System**, it follows the device's light or dark setting, also while a book is open.

Pages with a fixed layout, such as comics and PDFs, keep their own colors, with the reader's background around them.
