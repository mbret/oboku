The reader part of oboku is built upon [https://prose-reader.com/](https://prose-reader.com/) and its [https://doc.prose-reader.com/react-reader](https://doc.prose-reader.com/react-reader/introduction).

Being the same maintainer for both project, one benefit the other and are usually developed in turn.

## Bookmarks, highlights and notes

### Bookmark a page

Tap the top right corner of a page. A marker shows in that corner. To remove the bookmark, tap the corner again.

### Highlight a passage and add a note

1. Select the text.
2. Tap **Annotate**.
3. Pick a color. Type a note if you want one.
4. Tap **Highlight**.

To change the color or the note, tap the highlight, make the change, then tap **Update**. To remove the highlight, tap it, then tap **Remove**.

### Find your bookmarks and highlights

Open the reader's menu, then tap **Bookmarks** or **Annotations** in the bottom bar. Both open the same dialog, with a tab for the bookmarks and a tab for the highlights. Tap a row to go to its place in the book.

Your bookmarks, highlights and notes sync between the devices of your account. They show in the reader only.

### Limits

- [KOReader progress sync](/guides/koreader-progress-sync) syncs the reading position only. Your bookmarks and highlights stay in oboku.
- A bookmark or a highlight points to a place in the book's file. If the file changes, it can point to the wrong place. See [Strong reliance on file content & structure](/guides/resources-matching-and-sync#strong-reliance-on-file-content-and-structure).
- A preview, such as the one the **Test** button of the optimize flow opens, shows no bookmarks or highlights and saves none.
