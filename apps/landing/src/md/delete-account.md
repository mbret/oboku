# Delete your oboku account

You can delete your oboku account and its data at any time, from the web app or from the Android app.

## Delete it in the app

1. Open [app.oboku.me](https://app.oboku.me) or the oboku Android app, and sign in.
2. Open **Profile**.
3. Select **Delete my account**.
4. Type `delete my account` to confirm, then select **Delete my account**.

## What is deleted

The deletion happens at once and cannot be undone. It removes:

- your account and its email address;
- your library: books, collections, tags and reading progress;
- your data sources, and the connections to Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive and other services;
- your OPDS servers and your KOReader sync data;
- the covers stored for your books and collections.

oboku does not delete the book files in your own storage, such as Google Drive or Dropbox: they stay yours.

The device you delete from also removes its copy of your library. Your other devices keep their copy until you sign out on them or clear the app's data.

## If you cannot sign in

Send an email to [bret.maxime@gmail.com](mailto:bret.maxime@gmail.com) from the address of your account, and ask for its deletion. We delete the same data as listed above, and reply when it is done.

This page is for accounts on app.oboku.me. For an account on a self-hosted oboku server, ask the person who runs that server.
